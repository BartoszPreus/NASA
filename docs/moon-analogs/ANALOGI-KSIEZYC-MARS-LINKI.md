## NASA resources
### Earth
1. **NASA Earthdata Search**: the main portal for searching and downloading NASA Earth observations (here: ICESat-2, GEDI, MODIS LST, DEMs)
   https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/
2. **NASA Worldview**: interactive browsing of NASA satellite imagery
   https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/index.html

### Moon and Mars
3. **NASA Planetary Data System (PDS)**: long-term archive of data from orbiter, lander and rover missions (here: MOLA for Mars)
   https://pds.nasa.gov/
4. **NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO)**: LROC, **LOLA (LiDAR)**, Diviner, LEND, Mini-RF and others
   https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/
5. **NASA LRO Diviner Lunar Data**: lunar surface temperatures, polar and resource products (among others Rock Abundance, Polar Cold Patch)
   https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/diviner.htm
