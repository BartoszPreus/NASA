Notes
- Haworth: the gazetteer page lists three control networks (-86.90 / -4.00 E primary, D = 35 km; -87.20 / -7.49 E and -87.45 / -5.17 E, D = 51.42 km). The primary value is used; older lists give -87.4 / 5 W.
- de Gerlache: primary network -88.48 / -88.34 E (= 271.66 E); the research notes had -88.5 / 87.1 W from another network.
- The two LPSC sites and the Nobile Rim 2 point are proposed landing points inside NASA candidate regions, not region centres; NASA publishes no region centre coordinates.
