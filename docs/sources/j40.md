  https://wvs.earthdata.nasa.gov/api/v1/snapshot (NASA Worldview Snapshots, GIBS imagery). 30/30 images, 0.88 MB.

  into the worktree's cache before the run; 204 responses were new downloads.

  `HLS_L30_Nadir_BRDF_Adjusted_Reflectance`, through the same Snapshots API, box 0.24 deg of latitude (about 26 km),
  600 px, dates every fourth day of three local summer months, the clearest wins (stop below 5% cloud-white plus
  no-data pixels). 30/30 images, 1.28 MB. Product page https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/lpcloud-hlss30-2.0
  returned HTTP 403: the HLS citation and DOI are UNVERIFIED.

## Place names

OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse geocoding at zoom 10 and 6 for all 30 sites, and forward search for Tso Kar, Hanle,
Campo de Piedra Pómez, Ojos del Salado, Salar de Maricunga and Lake Hazen (https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/,
data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Distances in `novelty_check_strict.csv` are great-circle distances from the
site centroid to those Nominatim points. Forward searches for "Pampa de La Joya" and "Llullaillaco" returned places in
Bolivia and Córdoba and were not used.

**Border case, strict rank 25 `S28W069-01` (27.3830 S, 68.9321 W).** The ranking says Argentina, OpenStreetMap
Nominatim says Copiapó, Atacama Region, Chile. The ranking's `country` comes from the Natural Earth 1:50m outlines
v5.1.2 (`pipeline/sites.py` `CountryLookup`, https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson):
the point lies inside the Argentina polygon and about 0.05 km (planar distance in degrees times 111 km) from the
Chile-Argentina outline, which at 1:50m is simplified by far more than that. The site straddles the border at this
resolution; the data keep what the code gives (Argentina). Checked 15 Nov 2026, after the novelty fill of issue #26.
