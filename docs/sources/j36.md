## Rules and borrowed definitions

- Ground scale of the side-by-side view: degrees to km on a sphere of radius 6371.0088 km (the radius `app/build_data.py` uses for `known_match.distance_km`, SCHEMA.md). The GIBS image (600 x 600 px over `image_bbox`, equal steps in degrees) is drawn at its ground proportions; the lunar hillshade covers the whole `window_m` (`pipeline/moon_reference.py · hillshade_png`, "plain grey hillshade of each window"). Both panels share one frame size and one scale bar length.
- Copernicus DEM tile URL in the site package: the rule `COP_URL` in `pipeline/io_earth.py` (`https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.amazonaws.com/{stem}/{stem}.tif`); checked with an HTTP HEAD for `N82W026` (200 OK, 15 Nov 2026). Registry: https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/
- Strict-mask match: centroid distance at or below `landcover_sensitivity.json · full_run.match_km`; the strict ranking is not joined by site id (its `note`).
- Colours: Okabe-Ito categorical colours (Okabe and Ito, "Color Universal Design", https://jfly.uni-koeln.de/color/) for the known-analog tags and control tiles, each with its own dash pattern; colour bars of the lunar maps use the matplotlib colour maps named in `instrument_info.*.cmap` (https://matplotlib.org/stable/users/explain/colors/colormaps.html). Neither colour page was opened in this job: the two URLs are UNVERIFIED; the hex stops are the commonly published values.
- Map library: Leaflet 1.9.4 (BSD-2-Clause), unchanged: https://leafletjs.com/
