## Per resource: why unused, what was found, how it is read

**JAXA G-Portal (challenge link 11).** Unused because downloads from G-Portal itself need an account.
The JAXA Earth API (https://data.earth.jaxa.jp/en/ , catalogue
https://data.earth.jaxa.jp/stac/cog/v1/catalog.json , 112 collections on 14 Nov) serves G-Portal products
without login as cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs. Used:
- `JAXA.G-Portal_GCOM-C.SGLI_standard.L3-LST.daytime.v3_global_monthly` and `...nighttime...`: land surface
  temperature of the SGLI instrument on GCOM-C, monthly, year 2021. The catalogue gives the citation "Japan
  Aerospace Exploration Agency. 2018. GCOM-C/SGLI L3 Map Land surface temperature (LST) (1-Month,1/24 deg)."
  and doi:10.57746/EO.01gs73bfs5sbzhyxg4j9ytajsa. Values are stored as uint16 with slope 0.02 K.
- `JAXA.G-Portal_GCOM-W.AMSR2_standard.L3-SMC.daytime.v3_global_monthly-normal` and `...nighttime...`: soil
  moisture content of AMSR2 on GCOM-W, monthly normals 2012-2021, in percent (int16, slope 0.1).
- Files are tiles of 90 x 90 degrees at the finest level, read by rasterio through `/vsicurl/` one pixel at a
  time; the catalogue item of each tile gives the file name and the conversion (`je:rasters`).
- Terms read on https://earth.jaxa.jp/en/data/policy/ : "the user is required to indicate its sources (JAXA and
  the research name)", commercial use is free "however, the user needs to notify JAXA thereof in advance", and
  "The copyrights of the products provided in the Service are the property of JAXA". The catalogue's licence
  label is "proprietary". The exact credit line the page proposes was not copied: UNVERIFIED until J25.

**NASA MODIS land surface temperature (challenge link 1).** `data/sources.csv` had it as "needs login, not
downloaded". The same JAXA catalogue hosts `NASA.EOSDIS_Terra.MODIS_MOD11C1-LST.<daytime|nighttime>.v061_global_monthly-normal`
(monthly normals 2012-2021, 0.05 degree), with the citation "Wan, Z., Hook, S., Hulley, G. (2021). MODIS/Terra
Land Surface Temperature/Emissivity Daily L3 Global 0.05Deg CMG V061. NASA EOSDIS Land Processes DAAC.
https://doi.org/10.5067/MODIS/MOD11C1.061". LP DAAC's own citation page was not opened: UNVERIFIED.

**ESA Open Science Catalog (challenge link 10).** Unused because it is a catalogue of research products and
most are regional. Its machine-readable form (https://esa-earthcode.github.io/open-science-catalog-metadata/ ,
366 products, 1,252 variables) was searched: the variables "Dunes", "Surface Roughness", "Land Surface
Temperature", "Permafrost" and "Thermal Conductivity" have no product; "Permafrost Area Fraction" has one,
`esa-cci-permafrost` ("Combined Permafrost Dataset ... Ground Temperature & Freezing/Thawing Rate", yearly
2000-2019, bounding box 16 N to 90 N), whose access link is the public store
`s3://deep-esdl-public/esa-cci-permafrost-1x1151x1641-1.0.0.zarr`. Used: `PFR` (permafrost area fraction,
percent) for every site and `T2m` (ground temperature at 2 m, degrees Celsius) for four chunks, year 2019, on
the 926.6 m grid in "WGS 84 / Arctic Polar Stereographic". The store is zarr v2 with blosc compression; the
module reads single chunks over HTTPS and decodes them with `numcodecs` (new line in `requirements.txt`),
because GDAL in the rasterio wheel has no blosc. A `T2m` chunk is 10 to 12 MB, a `PFR` chunk under 0.6 MB.
Coverage as probed: Devon Island 100%, Svalbard 100%, Putorana 100%, Kunlun (36.5 N) 100%, Yakutsk 86%, Askja
29%; no value at 82.5 N in north Greenland and at 80 N on Ellesmere Island (why was not established); nothing
south of the grid. Licence label in the catalogue: "proprietary"; the product's own terms were not opened:
UNVERIFIED.

**CSA RADARSAT-1 (link 7), RCM (link 6), SAR tutorial (link 8).** Not in the data layer. Facts for job J27:
the public bucket `https://radarsat-r1-l1-cog.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/` lists years 1996-2013; a ScanSAR
wide GeoTIFF opened in 2.4 s (9,600 x 10,560 pixels, uint8, 50 m spacing, 9 control points in EPSG:4326, CEOS
tags), over eastern Algeria; file names carry no position; the archive's search service answered HTTP 503; the
CSA dataset pages gave a certificate error from this machine. RCM data need an EODMS account; the tutorial
repository says its sample is RCM imagery of British Columbia fires. Use proposed: radar texture of the sites
next to the Mini-RF images of the lunar regions (J27), and compact polarimetry against Mini-RF (J28, needs a
person).

## What the first run says (118 sites: top 60 ranked, 30 largest flagged, 28 best known analogs)
No tile read failed (240 reads, about 95 s). The columns hold: both temperature sensors 118 of 118 sites, soil
moisture 102, permafrost fraction 56, ground temperature 47 (4 of 8 chunks read).

Medians by group. The means and the day-night difference use only months that have both a day and a night
value: 12 months at low latitude, 5 to 7 months (spring and autumn) for the Arctic sites.

| | ranked (56 of 60 north of 60 N) | flagged dune fields (27 of 30 within 30 degrees of the equator) | known analogs (24 of 28 within 30 degrees) |
|---|---|---|---|
| JAXA SGLI day mean / night mean [K] | 276.1 / 272.8 | 317.0 / 293.8 | 303.1 / 270.2 |
| JAXA SGLI day-night difference [K] | 2.8 | 23.0 | 31.6 |
| JAXA SGLI coldest monthly night mean [K] | 264.3 | 284.6 | 267.1 |
| NASA MODIS day-night difference [K] | 4.6 | 21.2 | 31.7 |
| JAXA AMSR2 soil moisture [%] | 4.5 | 7.0 | 4.3 |
| ESA permafrost fraction [%] (sites with a value) | 100 (51 sites) | 93 (2 sites) | 100 (3 sites) |
| ESA ground temperature at 2 m [deg C] | -6.8 (47 sites) | no value read | no value read |

- **The two sensors agree.** Over the 118 sites the day-night difference of JAXA SGLI (2021) and of NASA MODIS
  (normals 2012-2021) correlate with r = 0.977, median difference -0.02 K. The day means correlate with
  r = 0.983 but differ by a median of 8 K: the months behind them differ, so do not mix the two in one column.
- **How far from the Moon.** The coldest monthly night mean among the 60 ranked sites is 235 K (243 to 247 K
  at the north Greenland sites, 82 N, of the top twelve). The Diviner medians of annual mean temperature in the
  lunar regions are 51 K (Haworth) to 141 K (Malapert massif), 83 K in the 160 km polar window: even the
  warmest of them is 94 K below the coldest Earth value. This is a number for "where the analogy breaks", not
  a similarity.
- **Cold and ice-bearing ground.** Of the 60 ranked sites 51 have a permafrost value: 45 of them 100%, the
  lowest 57%. Ground at 2 m is between -17.4 and -0.1 deg C (47 sites, median -6.8). Nine ranked sites have no
  value, six of them north of 80 N.
- **The dune-field flag and temperature.** Within 30 degrees of the equator the flagged dune fields have a
  SMALLER day-night difference (23.0 K, 27 sites) than the known analogs (32.0 K, 24 sites), and wetter
  microwave soil moisture (7.0% against 4.3%). The groups also differ in elevation (the known analogs there are
  high volcanoes and the Atacama) and the microwave retrieval is known to be uncertain over sand, so this is an
  observation, not a confirmation of the flag. Job J26 tests it with controls.

## Limits
One pixel per site (2.6 km SGLI, 5.6 km MODIS, about 11 km AMSR2 grid) against sites of a few kilometres;
clear-sky skin temperature; one year of SGLI; a modelled permafrost product; ground temperature for half of the
chunks; nothing of this enters the terrain score. Reruns are cheap: every sampled value is cached in
`data/raw/partner_context/samples.json` (git-ignored).
