## Why this closes the job
- A public account gives detected GRD/GCD products only, from which the circular polarisation ratio cannot be formed, and only for scenes entirely inside Canadian land borders; a scene around Haughton on Devon Island, an Arctic island, would most likely include coastal waters (not checked scene by scene, because the search was down).
- The open MLC collection (which has the RR/RL/covariance channels) had no item over Devon Island or the Canadian top sites.
- A vetted account cannot be obtained before the deadline of the job (Sunday 08:00).
