# J33 sources: moss and lichen sensitivity of the land-cover mask (shortened version)

Job: GitHub issue #19. Code: `validation/landcover_sensitivity.py`, group `bare_strict` in `pipeline/landcover.py`.
Results: `data/validation/landcover_sensitivity.json`, `data/validation/landcover_sensitivity/`.

**Shortened version.** Run on the 67 one-degree tiles that hold the top 100 sites of `data/sites/ranking.csv`, not on
all 2,060 scanned tiles. It shows which default top sites fail a strict mask; it cannot show which other sites would
move up into the top 30, nor the new rank of a survivor. The full run (bare_strict for every tile, the sites stage
as a variant) is left open on #19.

## Sources

- ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200, read remotely from https://registry.opendata.aws/esa-worldcover-vito/
  (`https://esa-worldcover.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/v200/2021/map/...`), as by the scan.
  Licence, quoted from https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access (opened 15 Nov 2026): "The ESA WorldCover
  product is provided free of charge, without restriction of use." The page points to the Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0 International licence for the full terms.
- ESA WorldCover Product User Manual v2.0,
  https://esa-worldcover.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/v200/2021/docs/WorldCover_PUM_V2.0.pdf (opened 15 Nov 2026,
  legend table on page 15), class definitions quoted:
  - 60 Bare / sparse vegetation: "Lands with exposed soil, sand, or rocks and never has more than 10 % vegetated
    cover during any time of the year"
  - 100 Moss and lichen: "Land covered with lichens and/or mosses. Lichens are composite organisms formed from the
    symbiotic association of fungi and algae."
  So class 100 is vegetated ground by the product's own definition; class 60 is the one that matches "bare".
- MODIS MCD12Q1 v061 IGBP land cover through NASA GIBS (class 16 Barren), job J17: sources and colour map in
  `pipeline/landcover.py` and `docs/sources/j17.md`; the cells come from `data/earth/cells_nasadem/*.parquet`.

Libraries: numpy, pandas, scipy (ndimage.label), rasterio, pypdf (only to read the manual's legend page).
