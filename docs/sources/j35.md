# J35: literature check, second pass: analog catalogues and Russian-language sources (issue #21, 15 Nov 2026)

Written with SI assistance (Claude Code). Every verdict here was made by an SI model from the pages listed; a person
has not checked them, and no Russian reader has confirmed the Russian searches (issue #11). Outputs:
`data/sites/novelty_check.csv` (J22 rows), `data/validation/landcover_sensitivity/novelty_check_strict.csv` (J40
rows), summary in `docs/NOVELTY.md` sections 2, 3, 4 and 7. Regenerated: `data/sites/ranking.csv`,
`data/sites/sites.geojson`, `data/validation/landcover_sensitivity/ranking_bare_strict.csv`,
`sites_bare_strict.geojson`, `data/catalog.csv`, `data/README.md`, `data/run_log.csv`, `app/data/`.

Rule (as J22 and J40): a hit counts only if its page was opened and the sentence is quoted; search snippets and pages
that did not open are UNVERIFIED and change nothing. `mentioned` = an opened page names the region (not the site) as
an analog. The negative result is "not found in the analog catalogues and searches listed here", not "unknown".
