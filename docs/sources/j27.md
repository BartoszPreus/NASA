# J27 — RADARSAT-1 and Mini-RF texture

This comparison adds an Earth radar view to the site evidence. It uses the [RADARSAT-1 footprint index](../../data/reference/radarsat1_footprints.parquet), the [site manifest](../../data/sites/radarsat1_index.csv) and the [Earth–Moon figure](../figures/radar_earth_vs_moon.png).

## Archive and site selection

The EODMS STAC request returned HTTP 503. The [AWS bucket](https://registry.opendata.aws/radarsat-1/) listed **22,687 SCW and SGF keys**. The [index run](https://github.com/witczakw/moon-scan-runner/actions/runs/43659186559) produced **22,456 footprints (98.98%)**, from 1996-03-05 through 2013-03-29 by filename date. The [failure list](../../data/reference/radarsat1_footprints.failures.json) records 230 HTTP 416 responses and one scene without usable WGS84 control points. Header requests read byte ranges from the COGs. The index stores the convex hull of each scene's ground control points as hex WKB. Longitudes can extend beyond ±180° for scenes that cross the antimeridian.

The target list combines the first 20 unflagged sites in the published ranking, the ten flagged sites with the largest `area_km2` in the full GeoJSON, and the highest `best_window_km2` site from each of the ten tiles in the J18 target comparison. Overlap leaves **36 distinct requested sites**. A footprint intersected the centre of **11 of 36**. None of the ten largest flagged sites had a matching footprint. Ground control hulls indicate possible coverage. The image manifest distinguishes a centre match from a usable window. [J18 target comparison](j18.md)

The finder tries up to ten scenes per location, preferring SCW and then an incidence angle closest to 35°. `date` and `filename_utc` are tokens parsed from the filename. `ceos_acquisition_time` preserves the product's reported acquisition time. The two recorded dates differ on 491 footprints with both values, so use the CEOS field for acquisition timing. Scene-level incidence is metadata, and the angle at the site can vary across a swath.

## Measured coverage

The [quicklook run](https://github.com/witczakw/moon-scan-runner/actions/runs/43660114061) saved **8 of 36 requested site windows**, four ranked sites and four J18 references. Of the remainder, 25 had no matching footprint. Three Greenland sites had matching footprints, but every available scene fell short of 90% usable window pixels. The manifest records the individual outcomes in `status`, `n_scenes`, and `rejected`. Grand Erg Oriental is an additional figure example with `roles=figure_only` and is excluded from site coverage.

| Earth panel | Manifest `site_id` | Product | Usable pixels |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Grand Erg Oriental | `grand-erg-oriental` | SCW | 99.99% |
| Mauna Loa | `N19W156-01` | SGF | 90.82% |
| Southern Peary Land | `N82W026-01` | SGF | 97.45% |

The figures in the last column are `valid_share` in the manifest. The lunar windows were 338 × 338 pixels at about 118.45 m per pixel, with 100.0% usable pixels at Haworth and 91.2% at Connecting Ridge. Those figures come from `lunar_windows()` in the same workflow log. The figure marks missing samples in blue-grey.

## Texture and display

The quicklooks warp source DNs through the GeoTIFF ground control points onto a local 50 m grid. Windows span 40 by 40 km. Zero fill and pixels outside the swath are masked, and a window needs at least 90% usable pixels. `cv_dn` is the standard deviation divided by the mean of the valid DNs. `power_share_200m_2km` is the fraction of non-DC power in the 200 m to 2 km radial wavelength band after mean removal, zero filling masked residuals and applying a separable Hann taper. These are descriptive Earth image statistics.

SCW inputs in the index are 8-bit and the ordinary SGF inputs are 16-bit. One SGF footprint has a complex sample type, which the quicklook code skips. The figure sets the same nominal projected extent for the Earth and lunar windows, with different pixel spacings. RADARSAT-1 uses C band and the plotted DNs are uncalibrated. The Moon panels use the S-band Mini-RF S1 mosaic at about 118.45 m per pixel. All panels are independently stretched to 8-bit greyscale. View them for spatial texture. Illumination geometry, speckle, local incidence and the different sensors and processing limit interpretation. Radar brightness and polarisation are outside the comparison.

## Reproduce

Run the [workflow](../../cloud/radarsat1-index.yml) in the private runner. With no `footprint_run` input it reads six disjoint year shards and combines them. Each shard uses 16 threads and appends successful reads and errors to a JSONL checkpoint under `data/raw/radarsat1/`. A later index run retries failed headers and reuses successful records. Supply the completed run ID as `footprint_run` to render the current team `ranking.csv`, `sites.geojson` and lunar reference against that footprint index. Copy `pipeline/radarsat1.py`, its `pipeline` dependencies and those three tracked inputs to the runner before dispatch. Run the same two product stages from the repository root with the committed index:

```sh
python -m pipeline.radarsat1 find
python -m pipeline.radarsat1 quicklook
```
