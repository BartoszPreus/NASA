## Why 41 scanned tiles have no row in `data/earth/tiles/tile_summary.csv`

`data/earth/tiles/tiles_scanned.csv` marks 2,101 tiles done (2,023 `done`, 78 `cached`); `tile_summary.csv` has
2,060 rows. The 41 missing tiles are all `done`, none has an error, and together they hold 39,088 cells
(`n_cells` of the scan log, equal to the `n_cells` column of `tiles_scanned.csv` for every one of them).

How a tile reaches `tile_summary.csv`:
1. `pipeline/cells.py` (`earth_cells_for_tile`) marks a cell `valid` when at least 99% of its pixels are finite
   and its slope at 240 m is above 0.02 deg (`MIN_FINITE_FRAC`, `MIN_LAND_SLOPE_DEG`): flat sea and flat ice are
   not valid.
2. `pipeline/earth_scan.py` (`merge_cells`) writes only valid cells with water under 0.5 and bare at least 0.5 (or
   no land-cover data) into the committed `data/earth/cells/*.parquet`.
3. `pipeline/sites.py` builds `tile_summary.csv` with a `groupby("tile")` over the scored cells, so a tile with no
   kept cell has no row.

The scan logs of the cloud runs (`log_cloud_43623650020.csv` and the shard logs `log_s<i>of20.csv` of run
43623650020, local copies under `data/raw/`, not in git) give `n_valid` 0 to 5 for each of the 41 tiles: 25 valid
cells in all, 28 tiles with none. No cell row of any of the 41 tiles is in the committed cell tables (checked over
all 22 files of `data/earth/cells/`). 37 tiles are Arctic (Greenland, the Canadian Arctic islands, Svalbard,
Franz Josef Land, Severnaya Zemlya and others at 62-82 N), two are South Georgia (S54W038, S55W039), one is in
French Polynesia (S11W140) and one in the Red Sea (N23E036). Which of the two conditions (validity or the
water/bare filter) removed the 25 valid cells is not recorded, because the per-tile caches are not in git.
Nothing in the data was changed; `docs/VALIDATION.md` (first paragraph) and the note of `app/data/scan_plan.json`
now say this.

## Other housekeeping
- 59 of the 119 JPEGs in `data/sites/images/` were not in `index.csv` (images of earlier rankings; the producer
  `pipeline/nasa_context.py` writes new images but never deletes old ones). Only the generated catalogue named
  them; they were removed. 60 images remain, all in `index.csv`.
- `data/earth/tiles/SCAN_STATUS.md` ("Scan tiles finished: 5 of 2023", 14 Nov 18:41) was removed with its
  catalogue entry: its producer `pipeline/checkpoint.py` is the superseded local scan loop and needs the local
  per-tile caches.
