# J31: why each known analog was chosen (issue #17, 15 Nov 2026)

Written with SI assistance (Claude Code). Every classification below was made by an SI model from the pages listed
here; a person has not checked them. Output: `data/reference/known_analogs_selected_for.csv` (68 rows: every
non-operational row of `data/reference/known_analogs.csv`, joined by name, lat, lon). Used by
`validation/effectiveness.py` (`recovery.by_selected_for`, `recovery.strict_mask`) and `validation/enrichment.py`
(`by_selected_for`, `strict_mask`); result in `docs/VALIDATION.md`, section "J31".

**Deviation from the issue.** The issue asked for a new column `selected_for` in the shared
`data/reference/known_analogs.csv` after a one-hour wait for objections. The conductor waived the wait but decided
that the shared file stays unchanged: the classification lives in its own file, joined by name, lat and lon.

## How pages were read

- Every `source_url` of a non-operational row was downloaded once on 15 Nov 2026 with Python `urllib`
  (User-Agent `zodiak-validation-script/1.0`, no browser spoofing, TLS verification on), converted to plain text and
  searched for the sentences that say why the place is used as an analogue. Quotes are copied from that text.
- Where the `source_url` page gives no reason, at most one more page was opened (a programme page: BASALT, ESA
  Pangaea, AMASE, Desert RATS). Search-engine snippets were read only to find such a page and are never used as
  evidence. A page that did not open, or that gives no reason, gives `unknown`.
- Not done: the challenge page (https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/permanent-moon-base-locations-and-mars/)
  failed TLS verification from the venue network (urllib and curl: untrusted root); the check was not switched off.

## Rules

- `terrain`: the quoted reason is the shape of the ground or work on it: relief, landforms, a surface similar in
  form, rover, EVA or lander tests (for example "train for moonwalking", "Lunar Electric Rover" tests, a crater field
  built to copy part of Mare Tranquillitatis).
- `material`: rock type, mineralogy, impact geology, geology training (Apollo geology field trips, ESA Pangaea,
  impact-geology training sites).
- `environment`: climate, aridity, cold, radiation, "surface conditions".
- `other`: exobiology, instrument tests, operations inside a lava tube.
- `unknown`: no opened page states a reason.
- One class per row, the reason the page puts first. `terrain_also_named = yes` marks rows whose opened page also
  names the ground (terrain, topography, rover or EVA traverses) next to another reason; the scripts report the
  union `terrain_broad` (terrain, or terrain also named) as a sensitivity check.
- The class says why the site was chosen, not for which body: Devon Island, Schirmacher Oasis and the Lut Desert
  are `terrain` for **Mars** landforms.
