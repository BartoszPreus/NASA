# J42: context for the strict-mask ranking (issue #28, 15 Nov 2026)

Written with SI assistance (Claude Code). The strict-mask ranking of J33 (`data/validation/landcover_sensitivity/ranking_bare_strict.csv`,
WorldCover class 60 only) gets the context stages the default ranking has. No new module was copied: the existing
stages got options for another ranking and another output folder, their defaults unchanged (the default files under
`data/sites/` are byte-identical after the job).

## Rules of the comparison (`validation/strict_context.py`)

- Same rules for both top 30s: context rows matched by position within 3 km (every one of the 60 sites matched its
  own row, at most 0.7 km; the second-nearest row was at least 6.5 km away); temperature from NASA MODIS 12-month
  normals for every site (JAXA SGLI has partial years at polar sites, so the two groups would otherwise rest on
  different sensors); lunar value = Diviner `temp_avg_K.median` of the site's `best_region`.
- Permafrost: sites outside the ESA grid (southern hemisphere and south of about 16 N) are "no data", never 0.
- Three sites are in both top 30s (S21W070-01, N19W156-01, N81W072-01); their values come from the same cache keys
  and are identical in both tables (checked).

## Not done, and what can mislead

- No NASADEM variant in the strict rank uncertainty: NASADEM cells carry MODIS IGBP land cover, which has no
  counterpart of the class-60-only mask (`rank_uncertainty_strict.json` · `configuration.nasadem_variant`).
- POWER describes a cell of about 50 km whose elevation is 1.8 km below to 1.2 km above the strict top-30 sites
  (`nasa_context_strict.csv` · `power_grid_note`); its precipitation and skin temperature are those of the cell.
- Satellite land surface temperature is a clear-sky skin temperature; Diviner's value is the temperature 2 cm below the
  lunar surface. The difference measures how far the analogy breaks, not a quantity to be closed.
- The medians of the per-site lunar difference are not the difference of the medians: the strict sites match warmer
  lunar regions more often (`diviner_mean_k_of_best_region.median` 110.9 K against 109.3 K).
- App export (`app/data/strict_ranking.json`): ranks 1 to 100. To stay under the 25 MB budget, 26 of the 30 strict
  images are stored at 400 x 400 px, JPEG quality 75 (the other 4 are byte-identical to default images and are
  shared); `meta.json` · `strict_images` lists them.
