# Judging and awards (Judging & Awards Guide)
Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/ (as of 13 Nov 2026). Team notes, translated from Polish on 15 Nov 2026.

## Eligibility for Global Judging
- The project answers one of the official challenges.
- Every team member (including the Team Owner) is registered in **the same Local Event** (Poznań, not on the waitlist) and appears in the Members tab. Nobody can be added after submissions close. A team has 1-6 people.
- All fields filled in, a demo (at most 7 slides or a video of at most 30 s), everything in English.
- Both checkboxes ticked (Terms, and originality plus a full list of references).
- Screening for ties to China (NASA policy).

## 5 criteria (each on a 1-5 scale, at every stage)
| Criterion | What it is about | How to get a 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact** | How big a problem you solve and for how many people | Important problem, wide reach, inspiring, "could change the world" |
| **Creativity** | Originality, novelty or a substantial improvement | A breakthrough approach nobody has tried |
| **Validity** | Scientifically and technically feasible, works, useful | Ready to deploy, user-friendly |
| **Relevance** | Fits the challenge, **NASA data at the core** of the solution, not as an add-on | NASA data are key to the value of the project |
| **Presentation** | Storytelling, clarity, structure (demo plus the whole project) | A polished, engaging story |

> NASA says explicitly: teams polish the first four criteria and neglect the **presentation**. Time must go into the pitch and the story.

## Stages
1. **Local judging (Poznań)**: the local jury selects **Global Nominees**, announced **3 Dec 2026**. In Poznań there are also local and sponsor awards on site (gala on Sunday at 18:00).
2. **Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions**: **17 Dec 2026**.
3. **Global Winners**: executive committee of NASA and the partner agencies, announced **26 Jan 2027**, then a virtual gala.

## 10 global awards
- **Best Use of Science**: the best and most correct use of the scientific method.
- **Best Use of Data**: space data made accessible or used in a unique application.
- **Best Use of Technology**: the most innovative use of technology.
- **Galactic Impact**: the greatest potential to improve life on Earth.
- **Best Mission Concept**: the most credible concept and design.
- **Most Inspirational**: "captures our hearts".
- **Best Storytelling**: the most creative story about the potential of open data.
- **Global Connection**: connects people around the world through technology.
- **Art & Technology**: a combination of technical and creative skills.
- **Local Impact**: the greatest potential to change a local community. (Team note: a good target for a project aimed at Wielkopolska / Poland.)
