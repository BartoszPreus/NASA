## Important differences: local vs global
- Locally we submit **by Sun 13:30** (the global portal allows until 23:59, but in Poznań the jury judges from 14:30).
- On stage there is a **3 min pitch + 2 min Q&A**. That is not the same as the demo for the portal (at most 7 slides or a video of at most 30 s). Prepare both, preferably from the same deck.
